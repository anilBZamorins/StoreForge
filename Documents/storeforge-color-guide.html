Gradient conventions: diagonal gradients always run at 135° (top-left → bottom-right); the only vertical (180°) gradient is the admin sidebar. Two-stop gradients only — the darker stop leads, the lighter stop closes. When creating new banner gradients, pick the leading stop from the neutrals/semantic palette and close with Flame #FF5A36 or a brighter tint of the leading hue.

Rule of thumb: Flame #FF5A36 is the single shared action color across all three surfaces. Platform surfaces (website, admin) use the cool slate neutral ramp (#0F172A → #F7F8FB); tenant storefronts may swap in their own warm/branded neutral ramp (like Aura Living's #20201C → #FBF9F5) and accent, but status badge pairs and semantic colors stay consistent everywhere.
