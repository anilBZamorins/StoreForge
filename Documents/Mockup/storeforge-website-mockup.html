FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

Can I change plans later?
Yes. Upgrades apply immediately with prorated billing, and downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle.
What happens after my free trial ends?
You'll be prompted to add billing details to continue. Your store and data are kept safe during the trial period.
Do you support online payment gateways?
Phase 1 supports Cash on Delivery checkout for your customers. Online payment gateways are planned for a future release.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, you can cancel from your Store Admin Dashboard at any time — no long-term contracts.
